OUR WORKING METHODOLOGY

Our Assignment Approach

Every valuation assignment follows a structured and transparent process, beginning with understanding the client's requirements and concluding with a professionally prepared valuation report.

01 ◎

Understanding the Assignment

Establish clarity on the valuation purpose, asset class, scope of work, intended users and applicable reporting requirements.

  • Purpose and intended use of valuation
  • Nature and location of assets
  • Scope, timeline and deliverables
02 ▤

Document Review & Information Analysis

Examine the available records and technical information to understand the assets and establish the information base for the valuation.

  • Asset registers and machinery lists
  • Purchase invoices and ownership records
  • Make, model, capacity and technical particulars
03 ⚙

Physical Inspection & Technical Assessment

Where included in the agreed scope, inspect the assets and assess their observable condition and relevant physical characteristics.

  • Physical identification and verification
  • Condition, age and apparent wear
  • Photographic documentation and observations
04 ⌕

Valuation Analysis & Methodology

Analyse relevant market and cost information, consider appropriate valuation approaches and apply suitable methods in accordance with the assignment requirements.

  • Market, Cost and Income Approaches, as relevant
  • Depreciation and obsolescence considerations
  • Basis of value and premise of value
05 ✓

Review & Professional Evaluation

Review the valuation workings, supporting information, assumptions and limitations to ensure consistency between the analysis and the conclusions.

  • Verification of calculations
  • Review of assumptions and limitations
  • Consistency of valuation conclusions
06 ▣

Valuation Report & Delivery

Prepare and deliver a comprehensive valuation report setting out the assignment details, methodology, analysis, assumptions and final conclusions.

  • Structured valuation report
  • Supporting workings and relevant annexures
  • Delivery in accordance with agreed requirements
Our Commitment

Professional integrity, objectivity, technical diligence and transparency form the foundation of our valuation assignments. The extent of inspection, verification and analysis remains subject to the agreed scope of engagement.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a registered valuer?

A professional enrolled with IBBI for a specific asset class, authorised to carry out valuations required under the Companies Act and the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code.

When is plant and machinery valuation required?

Typically for insolvency and liquidation, bank lending and security, LIE reports of bank, mergers and acquisitions, asset sale or purchase, insurance and financial reporting.

What documents should I share for an initial discussion?

A machinery list, asset location, valuation purpose and a few photographs are a useful starting point. Further documents can be identified after understanding the assignment.

How is machinery value determined?

By the machine's make, capacity, age, condition and market demand, usually using the cost approach cross-checked with market evidence.

Do you do valuation for sale and purchase of machinery?

Yes. See our sale and purchase valuation page.

Do you accept assignments outside Punjab?

Enquiries from other locations in India are welcome. The feasibility, inspection requirements, scope and terms will be considered individually.
