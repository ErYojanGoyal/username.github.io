Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This website (plantmachineryvaluation.in) is operated by Er. Chandan Goyal, IBBI Registered Valuer (P&M), Jagraon, Punjab. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you use the enquiry form, we collect your name, phone number, email address (optional), the asset class you select and your message. If you call or message us on WhatsApp, we receive your number and the details you share.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, discuss and carry out valuation assignments, and keep records of communication. We do not sell your information.

Service providers

Form submissions are processed by Web3Forms and delivered to our email. The page includes an embedded Google Map. If we run Google Ads or analytics, Google may use cookies or similar technologies to measure visits and advertising results. These providers handle data under their own privacy policies.

Confidentiality of assignments

Documents and information you share for a valuation are treated as confidential and used only for that assignment, except where disclosure is required by law or by the terms of the engagement.

Retention and your choices

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed for the purpose above, and records of assignments as required by applicable rules. You may ask us to correct or delete your enquiry information by contacting us, subject to legal record-keeping requirements. You can also block cookies in your browser settings.

Contact

For any privacy question, call 9465353872 or use the enquiry form. We may update this policy from time to time, and the date above shows the latest version.
