(d) Interest or conflictThe valuer has no personal, financial or professional interest in the corporate debtor or its assets.

(f) Intended useValuation required under the Code and its regulations.
(g) Asset particularsLocation; owner or lessee; asset number; make and name; installed and operating capacity; year of manufacture and purchase; country of origin; date put to use; quantity and units, as per Annexure A.

(k) Basis and premise of valueFair value and liquidation value; premise chosen for each asset type, with reasons, in a table.
(l) Standards followedIBBI-notified valuation standards followed: [name the specific standards and paragraphs].
(m) Approaches and methodsMarket, income and cost approaches each considered. Cost approach adopted for machinery; market approach used as cross-check; income approach rejected as machinery cash flows cannot be separated. Methodology discussed with the CoC under Regulation 35(1)(b) of the CIRP Regulations: [date and minutes at Annexure].
(n) Discounts and premiumsDepreciation and obsolescence adjustments, and any liquidation discount, each with reasoning.
(o) Data and inputsMarket quotes, manufacturer data, portals and cost indices, with reasons for reliance.
(p) InspectionsPhysical check against the asset register on [date]; condition, wear, layout and working status observed; photographs taken.
(q) Sustainability and functional factorsPollution control, boiler, factory and fire approvals; legal compliance; whether imported or indigenous; supplier category.
