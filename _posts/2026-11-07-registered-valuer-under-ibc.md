title: "Role of a Registered Valuer under IBC"
description: "How registered valuers estimate fair value and liquidation value in corporate insolvency."
---
In corporate insolvency, registered valuers are appointed to estimate the fair value and liquidation value of the corporate debtor's assets, typically for each relevant asset class. These figures help the resolution professional and the committee of creditors compare resolution plans with liquidation outcomes.

## Fair value and liquidation value

Fair value reflects what assets could fetch in an orderly transaction between willing parties. Liquidation value reflects a forced or time-bound sale. A good report states the premise of value, the inspection findings and the standard followed.
