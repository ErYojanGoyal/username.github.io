title: "Role of a Registered Valuer under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), 2016"
description: "An overview of the role, responsibilities and professional obligations of a Registered Valuer in determining Fair Value and Liquidation Value under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), 2016"
---
The Registered Valuer plays a crucial role in the insolvency resolution process under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), 2016. Valuation provides an independent and objective assessment of the assets of a corporate debtor and supports informed decision-making by stakeholders.

Under the IBC framework, the Resolution Professional (RP) appoints Registered Valuers to determine the fair value and liquidation value of the corporate debtor in accordance with the applicable regulations. Such valuation must be conducted by valuers registered with the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI) under the relevant asset class.

The primary responsibility of a Registered Valuer is to ensure that the valuation is carried out independently, impartially and in accordance with the applicable valuation standards. The valuer examines relevant financial records, asset registers, technical documents and other supporting information. Where necessary, physical inspection of assets is also undertaken.

For Plant and Machinery, the valuer assesses the nature, condition, age, capacity, utility, obsolescence and marketability of the assets. Appropriate valuation approaches and methods are adopted based on the purpose of valuation and the characteristics of the assets.

Fair Value represents the estimated realisable value of the assets between knowledgeable and willing parties in an arm's-length transaction, while Liquidation Value represents the estimated realisable value when the assets are sold individually under liquidation conditions.

The Registered Valuer is also responsible for maintaining proper documentation, disclosing assumptions and limitations, and presenting the valuation results in a comprehensive valuation report.

Importantly, the valuer must maintain professional independence, confidentiality and ethical standards throughout the assignment. Any conflict of interest must be disclosed, and undue influence from any stakeholder must be avoided.

The valuation report assists the Committee of Creditors (CoC) in evaluating resolution plans and understanding the financial position of the corporate debtor. It also contributes to transparency, accountability and fairness in the insolvency resolution process.

Thus, a Registered Valuer acts as an independent professional whose work bridges technical asset assessment and financial decision-making under the IBC. An accurate, unbiased and well-supported valuation strengthens stakeholder confidence and contributes to the effective implementation of the insolvency resolution framework.

## Fair value and liquidation value

Fair value reflects what assets could fetch in an orderly transaction between willing parties. Liquidation value reflects a forced or time-bound sale. A good report states the premise of value, the inspection findings and the standard followed.
