To prepare an accurate and well-supported plant and machinery valuation report, we need certain documents and information from you. Having them ready saves time, avoids repeated follow-ups and reduces the assumptions we have to make in the report.

**Please note:** this is a general list. The exact requirement may vary from assignment to assignment, depending on the purpose of valuation, the type of assets and the information already available. We will confirm the specific list for your assignment when we start.

## 1. Basic details
- Name, address and contact person of the owner or company
- Purpose of valuation (IBC, bank loan, sale or purchase, insurance, financial reporting)
- Valuation date, and the name of the person or institution requesting the report
- Location of each asset and arrangements for site access

## 2. Asset records
- Fixed asset register showing date of purchase, cost and depreciation
- List of plant and machinery with make, model, serial number, capacity and year of manufacture
- Purchase invoices and purchase orders
- Import documents such as the bill of entry, for imported machinery
- Installation and commissioning records

## 3. Technical information
- Plant layout and process flow chart
- Manufacturer catalogues and technical specifications
- Maintenance and repair history, and details of major overhauls or upgrades
- Production capacity and current utilisation
- Operating status of each item: working, idle, under repair or scrapped
- Photographs of machines and nameplates, if available

## 4. Financial documents
- Latest balance sheet and fixed asset schedule
- Depreciation schedules
- Project report, for a new or expanding unit

## 5. Ownership and legal documents
- Proof of ownership of the assets
- Lease, hire-purchase or rental agreements, where machinery is not fully owned
- Details of charges or hypothecation on the assets
- Insurance policies
- Statutory licences and certificates relevant to the machinery, such as pollution control consents or boiler and pressure vessel certificates

## 6. Additional documents, depending on the purpose
**Insolvency (IBC):** insolvency commencement date, the appointment order or letter of the resolution professional or liquidator, the asset inventory prepared by them, and any earlier valuation reports.

**Bank loan:** sanction letter or terms, the bank's required format or scope of work, and any previous valuation report.

**Sale or purchase:** the offer or quotation, buyer and seller details, and the agreed basis of sale.

**Insurance:** current policy and sum insured, and the replacement basis required.

## What if some documents are not available?
Please tell us early. Missing documents do not always stop the assignment, but the report will then state clearly which assumptions were made and which information could not be verified. We rely on you to confirm facts about the machinery, since we work from the information you provide and from our inspection.
