---
title: "Three Approaches to Plant & Machinery Valuation"
description: "Cost, market and income approaches explained simply for plant and machinery valuation."
---
## Cost approach
Estimates replacement cost new and deducts physical, functional and economic obsolescence. It is the most common method for specialised machinery.

## Market approach
Compares similar assets recently sold or offered. It works well for standard, widely traded equipment.

## Income approach
Values an asset by the future earnings it can generate. It is used where machinery cash flows can be separated, such as power plants.
